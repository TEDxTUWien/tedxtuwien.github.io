Privacy policy

We process only as much personal data as is needed to run this website and the newsletter. There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising cookies and no external fonts or scripts. The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

TEDxTUWien - Verein zur Unterstützung innovativer Ideen, Theresianumgasse 27, 1040 Wien, Österreich
Email: office@tedxtuwien.at
Data protection contact: Reinhard Kletter

2. Visiting the website and hosting

The static pages are delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft, USA). When you visit, the provider processes your IP address and access data (server logs) as technically necessary to deliver the page and prevent abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Fonts are hosted locally. No cookies are set and no content is loaded from third-party servers. The only link to a third party that handles data is the newsletter form (Microsoft Forms), and only after you click the button (section 3).

3. Newsletter

Signing up: The “Subscribe” button opens a form of the association hosted with Microsoft Forms (part of the association’s Microsoft 365 account; Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd. / Microsoft Corporation). Nothing is sent to Microsoft until you open the form, and nothing is sent to us until you submit it. In the form you enter your email address and confirm your consent. The form tells our mailbox info@tedxtuwien.at about each new sign-up. Providing your email address is voluntary, but without it we cannot send you the newsletter. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 174 TKG 2021).

Processor: Microsoft processes the form data on our behalf under its data protection addendum (processor agreement within the Microsoft 365 contract). Transfers outside the EU/EEA, where they occur, rely on the EU standard contractual clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Microsoft’s own privacy information applies to the technical operation of the service.

Sending: We add your address to our mailing list by hand and send the newsletter from the association’s mailbox. You can unsubscribe at any time by writing to info@tedxtuwien.at; we then delete your address from the list and from the form responses. We keep the sign-up record only as long as needed to document your consent.

4. Speaker and team area

Speakers and team members can open a password-protected page with internal information (e.g. the schedule). The password is checked in your browser; it is not sent to us and we do not store any data about your login.

Cookies: No cookies are set — neither in the protected area nor on the public pages.

5. External links

Links to ticketing (Universe), YouTube and LinkedIn lead to third-party sites. Data is only transferred to those providers once you click such a link; their privacy policies apply.

6. Retention

Newsletter data is stored until you withdraw consent or ask for deletion. Security logs are kept for a limited time. Team member data is deleted once access is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Write to office@tedxtuwien.at.

If you believe the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde.

8. Changes

We update this policy when features of the website change. The version published here applies.
