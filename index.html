01 — About

A TED stage, built by the TU Wien community

TEDxTU Wien brings the global TED format to Vienna's University of Technology. Once a year, researchers, founders, artists and unexpected voices share ideas worth spreading — framed by exhibitions, performances and conversations that continue long after the stage lights dim.

The event is organized entirely by a volunteer team and operated under license from TED.

05 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

What is the difference between TED and TEDx? +

TEDx events are local, self-organized events that bring the TED experience to communities around the world — the x stands for "independently organized TED event". TEDxTU Wien is run by a volunteer team under license from TED.

When does TEDxTU Wien 2027 take place? +

On Sunday, 14 November 2027 — our 10th anniversary edition. Time, venue, theme and speakers are still to be announced. Join the newsletter to hear it first.

How do I get tickets? +

Ticket sales for 2027 have not started yet. Subscribe to the newsletter — we announce the release there first.

Where can I watch talks of previous editions? +

Recordings are published on the official TEDx YouTube channel of TEDxTU Wien. The recordings of the 2026 edition will follow there.

Can I speak at or partner with TEDxTU Wien? +

Yes — speaker nominations and partnership inquiries are open year-round. Reach out at office@tedxtuwien.at with your idea worth spreading.

Who organizes TEDxTU Wien? +

A five-person volunteer core team around organizers Fabian Holly, Reinhard Kletter, and Lisa Marx, supported by a wider circle of volunteers from the TU Wien community.
